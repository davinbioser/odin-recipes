Ayam Geprek Recipes

Description

Ayam geprek is a traditional Indonesian dish made of crispy, battered fried chicken that is crushed or mashed together with spicy sambal using a mortar and pestle. This popular dish combines the crispy texture of the battered skin with the savory flavor of the chicken and the fresh, spicy kick of the onion sambal.

Ingredients

  • Ingredients for Breaded Fried Chicken. Prepare 2 pieces of chicken (breast or thigh), half a lime, half a teaspoon of salt, and a quarter teaspoon of ground black pepper for the marinade.

  • Breading Ingredients. Use 5 tablespoons of medium-protein all-purpose flour, 1 tablespoon of cornstarch (for extra crispiness), half a teaspoon of bouillon powder, a quarter teaspoon of baking powder (optional), and about 50 ml of ice water.

  • Ingredients for Onion Sambal. Prepare 10 red bird’s eye chilies (adjust to your preferred level of spiciness), 3 cloves of garlic, half a teaspoon of salt, a quarter teaspoon of granulated sugar or mushroom broth, and 3 tablespoons of hot oil left over from frying the chicken.

  • Oil and Accompaniments. Prepare enough cooking oil for deep-frying, warm white rice, and sliced cucumber or cabbage as a side dish.

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken. Coat the chicken with lime juice, salt, and ground black pepper. Let it sit for about 15 minutes so the seasoning can soak in and the fishy smell disappears.

  2. Prepare the flour mixture. Combine the all-purpose flour, cornstarch, bouillon powder, and baking powder in a dry bowl. Transfer 2 tablespoons of the flour mixture to another bowl, then add ice water until it forms a slightly thick, wet batter.

  3. Coat and fry the chicken. Dip the chicken in the wet batter, then roll it in the dry flour while gently pinching it to create a crinkled coating. Fry in hot oil over medium heat until cooked through and golden brown, then drain.

  4. Make the onion sambal. Coarsely pound the red bird’s eye chilies, garlic, salt, and granulated sugar in a mortar. Pour the hot cooking oil left over from frying the chicken over the sambal mixture, then stir until well combined.

  5. Pound and serve. Place the breaded fried chicken in a mortar filled with sambal, then press or pound it with a pestle until the meat is slightly tenderized and the sambal coats it evenly. Serve immediately with warm rice and fresh vegetables.
