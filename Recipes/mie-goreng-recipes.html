Mie Goreng Recipes

Description

Mie goreng is a dish made with yellow noodles stir-fried with onions, sweet soy sauce, vegetables, and various protein sources such as chicken, eggs, and meatballs. This classic Indonesian dish is popular for its chewy texture and the perfect balance of savory, sweet, and slightly spicy flavors.

Ingredients

  • Main Ingredients. Prepare 1 package of dried egg noodles (about 140 to 200 grams), 2 chicken eggs, and 3 beef meatballs (thinly sliced).

  • Vegetables. Use 3 leaves of green mustard greens (chopped) and a handful of sliced fresh cabbage.

  • Chopped or Minced Spices. Prepare 4 cloves of shallots, 2 cloves of garlic, and 2 red bird’s eye chilies (optional, for a spicy flavor).

  • Sauces and Seasonings. Use 3 tablespoons of sweet soy sauce, 1 tablespoon of oyster sauce, half a teaspoon of salt, a quarter teaspoon of bouillon powder, and a quarter teaspoon of ground black pepper.

  • Oil and Garnishes. Prepare 2 tablespoons of cooking oil for sautéing, as well as fried onions and pickled cucumbers for serving.

Steps

  1. Boil the egg noodles. Bring a sufficient amount of water to a boil, then boil the egg noodles until tender but still chewy (don’t let them get too mushy). Drain the noodles and toss them with 1 teaspoon of cooking oil or sweet soy sauce to prevent them from sticking together.

  2. Sauté the spices and scramble the eggs. Heat 2 tablespoons of cooking oil, then sauté the shallots, garlic, and chili peppers until fragrant. Push the spices to the side of the pan, add the eggs, and scramble until cooked through.

  3. Add the meatballs and vegetables. Add the sliced beef meatballs, chopped Chinese cabbage, and cabbage to the pan. Stir briefly until the vegetables begin to wilt.

  4. Add the noodles and sauce ingredients. Pour the drained noodles into the skillet along with the sweet soy sauce, oyster sauce, salt, bouillon powder, and ground pepper. Stir constantly until the seasonings are evenly distributed and the noodles turn a glossy brown.

  5. Adjust the seasoning and serve. Taste the fried noodles to make sure the seasoning is just right, then remove them from the heat. Transfer to a serving plate and sprinkle with fried shallots before serving warm.
